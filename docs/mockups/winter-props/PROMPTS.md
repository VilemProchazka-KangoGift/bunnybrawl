# Prop concept generation prompts

Built-in image generation mode was used. The existing `winter-platforms/production-day.png` was supplied as a style and palette reference, not as a scene to repaint. The result is an art-direction sheet; the second prompt extracted a transparent atlas for fixture compositing. Neither image is production art yet.

## Concept sheet

> Use case: stylized-concept. Create a clean, high-quality character-free concept sheet for Winter Lake decorative props in a 2D storybook platform game. The attached screenshot is a STYLE AND PALETTE REFERENCE ONLY: match its dark hand-ink contours, turquoise translucent ice, pale snowy background, and playful cartoon proportions. Do not reproduce the game screenshot or its characters. Arrange THREE CLEARLY DIFFERENT prop families in three vertical columns on a quiet pale blue-gray background with ample white space and no labels or text. Each column shows: one full evergreen tree, one dense low snowy evergreen bush that can completely hide a small character (opaque connected foliage, no exposed branches), one small friendly snowman, one asymmetrical hand-built igloo, and a low snowball pile. Column 1: rounded plush boughs and soft uneven snow cushions, cozy but not Christmas-decorated. Column 2: wind-shaped irregular silhouette, sweeping boughs and rugged snow cuts, still friendly. Column 3: broad layered cedar fronds with icy teal facets and sparse frost, distinctive from the other two. Give every object believable hand-painted texture and strong controlled ink, subtle secondary strokes, no repeated geometric triangles, no vector-plastic gradients. Show whole objects isolated and separated, consistent three-quarter side-scrolling game view. Keep the large forms legible at about 50 pixels high. High craft, imaginative and nuanced.

## Transparent atlas

> Use case: background-extraction. Edit the attached three-column Winter Lake prop concept sheet. Remove ONLY the pale blue-gray background and ground shadows, making the empty space genuinely transparent. Preserve every illustrated prop exactly in place at full canvas size: all 15 items in the same five rows and three columns, with their original silhouettes, colors, ink outlines, details, and relative spacing. Do not add, merge, redraw, resize, shift, or alter any prop. Keep clean antialiased alpha edges without white halos. This output will be used as a transparent sprite atlas for a visual mockup.
