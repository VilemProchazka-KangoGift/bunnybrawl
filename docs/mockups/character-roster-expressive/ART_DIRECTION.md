# Expressive roster art direction

This pass uses the previous Pocket Plush drawings as **palette, ink, and material references**, while deliberately changing body shape and acting. Every sheet asks for eight full-body poses in a 4 × 2 grid: signature idle, two locomotion contacts, jump, sit, angry fast stomp, landing, and an attentive character beat. The prompt calls for transparent gutters, stable anatomy, and readable silhouettes at about 40 game pixels. The specific briefs below drive the differences; they are design intent, not a claim that the stills already animate well.

## Review questions

- Can the player tell the animal and the action from its silhouette at actual match size, without relying only on the face?
- Does a species trait cause the motion rather than appear as decoration attached to a shared pose?
- Do pose transitions keep foot contact, body volume, appendage roots, and facial landmarks stable?
- Do the wider tails, horns, quills, wings, and gills still fit the game’s visual bounds without implying a changed hitbox?
- Do quadruped or low-prowl explorations for Cat, Wolf, Tiger, and Rhino fit the roster’s movement language? Treat those as choices to validate in motion, not automatic replacements.
- Is the thin Axolotl readable against Meadow at night and behind the intended cover? Keep opaque bushes opaque.
