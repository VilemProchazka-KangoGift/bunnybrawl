# Silver Banks painted concept provenance

These three raster images were made with the built-in image generation tool. They are art-direction studies, not production gameplay art. The first two edit `polar-silver-banks-day.png`; the third edits the hand-inked concept to make a background-only asset. The matched `polar-silver-painted-{day,night}.png` captures scale that background to 1280 × 720 at 63% opacity behind the production renderer's platforms, props, characters, clouds, and day/night treatment.

On the lake, layered translucent blue ice, gentle cloudy marbling, a few naturally branching dark-blue cracks and pale frosty scuffs. Expressive but still lower contrast than playable elements. Strong material distinction between powdery snow and glazed ice. Preserve the screenshot's exact camera, 16:9 composition, all playable platforms and their positions, characters, trees, snowmen, igloo, snowballs, clouds, sun, and ground as faithfully as possible. Do not introduce new objects, characters, text, labels, UI, or gameplay geometry. No photorealism, no 3D render, no flat vector gradients, no thick black background outlines.

The result should be one clean continuous illustrated backdrop suitable for drawing game objects on top, with quiet open sky in the upper half and the lake unobstructed in the lower third.
